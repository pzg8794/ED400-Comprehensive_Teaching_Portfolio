# Proficiency Map

This map keeps the portfolio grounded in the rubric rather than in a generic teaching philosophy.

## Working evidence spine

1. **ED400A Dear Future-Teacher Me** - identity, professional purpose, CLD/neurodivergent commitments, algorithmic justice, burnout/boundaries, humane systems.
2. **ED400A trauma-informed reflection packages** - source-backed and transcript-grounded thinking about access, dignity, neurodivergent and CLD learners.
3. **TeachingPlacement Grade 4 Minecraft evidence** - real classroom routines, sign-in barriers, world-entry support, collaboration, troubleshooting, student excitement, and management decisions.
4. **ED452B Minecraft / Innovative Unit Plan** - unit design, UDL-like supports, assessment map, target-student profiles, computational-thinking goals.
5. **EDU442 identity/equity artifacts** - bias, self-portrait, educational identity, and professional reflection.

## Rubric-to-artifact mapping

| Proficiency | Core question | Artifact candidates | Draft move |
|---|---|---|---|
| Personal Statement | Who am I becoming as a teacher? | Dear Future-Teacher Me; trauma-informed reflection; EDU442 self-portrait | Connect identity, agency, access, dignity, and CS justice |
| 1. Learner Development | How do I support growth over time? | placement notes; differentiated tasks; developmental routines | Show developmental responsiveness |
| 2. Learner Diversity and Equity | How do I design for human variability and justice? | Minecraft unit; assessment map; placement evidence; ED400A/EDU442 reflections | Organize by 2.1, 2.2, 2.3 |
| 3. Learning Environments | How do I build safe, inclusive conditions? | sign-in safety, low-noise expectations, partner roles, debugging culture | Show routines and shared responsibility |
| 4. Content Knowledge and Application | How do I make elementary CS rigorous and meaningful? | Minecraft coding fundamentals, CSTA/ISTE/NYS alignment, cross-disciplinary work | Show CS as inquiry/problem solving |
| 5. Assessment of Learning | How do I know what students understand? | assessment maps, planning sheets, conferences, exit tickets, code artifacts | Separate access from understanding |
| 6. Planning for Instruction | How do I plan intentionally? | unit plan, lesson sequence, adaptation notes | Show backward design and flexibility |
| 7. Pedagogy | How do I teach effectively? | modeling, Driver/Navigator roles, Stop-Read-Predict-Run-Debug | Show routines that elicit thinking |

| 9. Reflection and Professional Growth | How do I learn from evidence? | ED400A annotations, design logs, transcript-grounded notes | Show revision and growth |
| 10. Advocacy and Ethical Leadership | How do I protect and advocate for learners? | policy/legal growth, privacy policy, algorithmic justice | Show ethical action and public responsibility |
