# Proficiency 8 — Communication and Collaboration

## Claim

I communicate clearly and collaborate productively with students, peers, and professional audiences, understanding that teaching is relational work that depends on dialogue, responsiveness, and shared meaning-making.

## Communication as Access

Communication in teaching is more than giving directions. It includes how expectations are framed, how feedback is delivered, how questions are invited, how ideas are translated for different audiences, and how relationships are sustained through language. My background across technical and educational fields has made this proficiency especially important to me because I regularly work between communities that do not always share the same vocabulary. I have had to learn how to make complex ideas legible without reducing them, and that skill transfers directly into teaching.

In classroom contexts, effective communication means clarity, tone, and responsiveness. Students need instructions that are understandable, goals that are visible, and feedback that supports rather than shames. In teacher-facing contexts, communication means presenting ideas in ways that colleagues can implement, question, and build upon. The AI PD module and roundtable materials gave me practice communicating professionally to teachers, while collaborative EDU442 work required me to co-construct materials with peers.

## Collaboration as Professional Practice

The Week 12 Group B activity showed how collaboration can deepen analysis when peers work together around a shared equity question. I contributed to a collective project examining inclusion and the construction of otherness in schools, and that experience reminded me that professional collaboration is both intellectual and interpersonal. It requires listening, negotiation, division of labor, and a willingness to revise one’s own ideas in relation to others.

In future teaching, I want collaboration to extend across students, colleagues, families, and communities. Computer science education becomes stronger when it is not isolated from the broader human relationships in the school.

## Artifact Evidence

- **[A6] EDU442 Week 12 Inclusion activity** — strongest evidence of peer collaboration and shared knowledge construction.
- **[A2] AI PD module and presentation** — demonstrates communication to a teacher audience.
- **ED400 podcast annotation / course discussion artifacts** — show analytical communication across educational media.

## Reflection

I see communication and collaboration as ongoing professional responsibilities. My growth is visible in how I now think more intentionally about audience, access, and shared understanding. I want to keep becoming a teacher who can communicate with clarity while also making room for dialogue and co-construction.
