# Public Copy: Trauma-Informed Reflection Summary

Source provenance: private ED400A trauma-informed teaching reflection packages.

## Core claim

The trauma-informed reflection work centers access, dignity, and support for neurodivergent and culturally/linguistically diverse learners. It treats student behavior and performance as information about the interaction between the learner and the learning environment, not as simple evidence of effort or compliance.

## Evidence pattern

The work connects:

- neurodivergent student experience;
- CLD student experience;
- trauma-informed and healing-centered teaching;
- anonymous/source-backed conversation notes;
- formal reflection about how teachers respond to student needs without exposing private information.

## Portfolio use

- Personal Statement: professional purpose and teacher identity
- Proficiency 2: equity, disability, language, culture, and institutional barriers
- Proficiency 3: safe and supportive learning environments
- Proficiency 9: reflective practice
- Proficiency 10: advocacy and ethical leadership
