# A5 — Trauma-Informed Teaching Reflection (ED400A)

**Course:** ED 400A — Teaching, Learning, and Schooling  
**Type:** Reflective Paper  
**Date:** Spring 2026

---

## Summary

This reflection engages with trauma-informed teaching frameworks as they apply to classroom practice. Drawing on practicum observations and personal narrative, the paper examines how trauma history, chronic illness, neurodivergence, and cultural background shape both students’ and teachers’ experiences in classroom environments. It connects directly to the “This I Believe” genre of the ED400 personal statement tradition.
