# A1 — Deconstructing Bias: From “Problem Student” to Contextual Learner

**Course:** EDU 442 — Schooling and Society  
**Type:** Reflective Paper (Academic, APA-style)  
**Date:** April 13, 2026  
**Instructor:** Dr. O’Neil-White

---

## Summary

This paper interrogates a specific bias — the reflex to read neurodivergent and culturally and linguistically diverse (CLD) students through a compliance-and-deficit lens. Drawing on four practicum evidence sets from an elementary placement at Pine Brook IGNITE (spring 2026) and three journal artifacts, the paper traces how the “problem student” category is produced through adult language, task design, and relational positioning — and how it dissolves when adults shift to co-regulation and competence-narrating moves.

The paper is autobiographically grounded: the author is a Dominican-American, first-generation, neurodivergent student teacher who learned of his neurodivergence one year before writing this paper. That positionality makes the analysis both personal and methodologically conscious.
