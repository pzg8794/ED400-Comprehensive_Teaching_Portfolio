# A2 — Teaching AI as a Learning Partner — Impacting Practice PD Module

**Course:** EDU 442 — Impacting Practice Project  
**Type:** Professional Development Module + Lesson Artifact + Presentation  
**Date:** Spring 2026  
**Placement:** Pine Brook IGNITE (Grade 3 AI lesson sequence)

---

## Summary

This project turns a Grade 3 AI lesson sequence from the Pine Brook IGNITE practicum placement into a professional development module for elementary teachers. The central routine is **Ask → Check → Choose → Change → Explain**. The module addresses AI access equity, student voice, authorship, privacy, and neurodivergent/CLD learner needs.

The project argues that AI use in schools is not socially neutral: without explicit routines, students may copy AI output, overtrust tool-generated language, share private information, or lose ownership of their ideas.
